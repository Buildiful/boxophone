Make some music.

Use your keyboard, tap the keys, or plug in a MIDI controller.

Computer keyboard

Press a number key from 1 to 7 to select an octave (for example, 4 selects octave 4). Press A, B, C, D, E, F, or G to play that natural note in the selected octave. Hold Space for sustain; [ and ] also shift octaves. Use the on-screen black keys for sharps and flats.

Mouse & touch

Hold white or black keys to play; slide while holding for glissando. Use the octave arrows on phones to reach more notes.

USB MIDI

Select Connect MIDI in Chrome or Edge and grant access. Connect one or more class-compliant MIDI keyboards. The first two can use independent octave ranges; additional controllers play their own note pitches.
