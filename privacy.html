Privacy

Boxophone by Buildiful® · Updated November 2026

Local music and input devices

Boxophone produces sound in your browser using Web Audio. Notes played via keyboard, mouse, touch, or connected USB MIDI controllers are processed locally. MIDI recording and export operate entirely within your device. The app does not upload performances, MIDI recordings, audio, or file contents to our servers.

If you choose to connect a MIDI controller, your browser may ask for permission to access compatible devices. The browser provides device names and MIDI messages to the page to enable playing; these device names and your note data are not intentionally sent to Analytics. You can disconnect hardware or revoke MIDI permission in your browser settings.

Optional Google Analytics

We use optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand aggregate tool usage. The Analytics script is not loaded unless you actively select Allow analytics. You may decline without losing any Boxophone features. If allowed, the app may record page views and high-level events such as instrument selected, keyboard octave changed, MIDI controllers connected (a count only), demo started, practice started/completed (song identifier), recording started/stopped, and MIDI exported. It does not intentionally report individual notes, timing of your performance, actual MIDI device names, recording content, or song files. Google may process technical information such as device details and IP address subject to its own policies.

Your preference is stored in browser local storage as buildiful_analytics_consent_v1 with a timestamp, and shared across tools on the same website origin. You can change it anytime via Analytics settings in the footer. If you withdraw permission after allowing Analytics, the page reloads to stop the tag. The app attempts to clear GA cookies accessible to this page, but other domain-scoped cookies may remain subject to browser settings.

Stored preferences

Your chosen instrument, octave ranges, volume, and split-keyboard preference may be saved in local storage as boxophone_settings_v1. Clear the site's browser storage to erase them.

External resources

The official Buildiful® logo and favicon files are bundled locally with this application. No third-party audio library or remote sound bank is required. Analytics is the only optional third-party tracking integration.
